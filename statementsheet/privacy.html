Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026

The short version. StatementSheet is not built yet. The only thing this website collects is the email address you give us, the pricing plan you clicked and the statement-volume range you picked. We use it to email you when early access opens. We don't sell it. There is no way to upload a bank statement to this website, and we will not ask you for client files until the product is live and you have read how they are handled.

Who we are

This website is operated by StatementSheet, ABN 79 395 679 698. You can reach us at francismike8384@gmail.com.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

When you submit the early access form, we collect:

  • Your email address — the only thing you type.
  • The plan you selected — Solo, Practice or Firm.
  • The volume range you selected — how many client bank statements you process in a month.

We do not ask for your name, your business name, your phone number, or any information about your clients.

Separately, like most websites, we use Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tracking. These place cookies and collect technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, and which advertisement brought you here. We use this to work out whether our advertising is worth running.

Why we collect it

  • To email you when early access opens for the plan you chose.
  • To understand which pricing plan and which size of practice is interested, so we build and price the product sensibly.
  • To measure the performance of our advertising.

We will not use your email address for unrelated marketing, and we will not add you to a general mailing list.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information or disclose it for anyone else's marketing. We do use these service providers:

  • Formspree — receives and stores form submissions on our behalf. Formspree is based in the United States, so your email address is stored overseas.
  • Google — Tag Manager, Analytics and Ads, for the measurement described above. Google processes this data overseas, including in the United States.
  • Our web host — serves this website and keeps standard server logs.

By submitting the form you consent to your email address being stored overseas by these providers. We cannot guarantee that overseas recipients handle information in the same way Australian law requires.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until early access opens and we have contacted you, or until you ask us to remove it — whichever comes first. If the product does not go ahead, we will delete the list and tell you we have done so.

Getting your information, correcting it, or having it deleted

Reply to any email from us, or write to francismike8384@gmail.com, and we will tell you what we hold, correct it, or delete it. There is no charge and you do not need to give a reason. We will action deletion requests within a reasonable time and normally within 30 days.

Cookies

The cookies on this website come from Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using Google's browser add-on. The early access form works with cookies blocked.

Security

This website is served over HTTPS, and form submissions are encrypted in transit. No website can be guaranteed completely secure, but the information we hold about you is limited to an email address, a plan choice and a volume range.

Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your personal information, email francismike8384@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and change the effective date above. If the change is significant — for example, when the product goes live and starts handling uploaded statements — we will email everyone on the early access list before it takes effect.
